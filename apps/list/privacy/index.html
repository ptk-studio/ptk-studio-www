Mochi List

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version. Your lists stay on your device: list names, items, notes, photos and checklist progress are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, and we never see them. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. The app sends anonymous usage figures and crash reports through Google's Firebase so we know what to fix, and this page says exactly what those are.

Who we are

Mochi List is made by PTK Ventures LLC. This policy covers the Mochi List app for iPhone and iPad, and this page. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@ptk-studio.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create: lists, items, notes, photos you attach, themes and checklist progress. It is stored in the app's own storage on your device, and deleting the app deletes it. Photos you attach are copied into the app; the app does not read the rest of your photo library.

When you export a list, the app makes a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; where it goes from there (Messages, Files, email) is up to you, under that app's policy. When you import a list, the app reads only the file you choose.

What the app sends, and why

  • App usage events to Google Firebase Analytics, e.g. "list created", "list played", "checklist completed", screen views, and when a session starts and how long it lasts, so we can see which features are used. Not linked to you.
  • Device and app information (device model, iOS version, app version, language, and a random app-instance identifier Firebase generates) to Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, to group usage and crash reports by version and device. Not linked to you.
  • Approximate location (country or region, worked out from your IP address; never GPS) to Firebase Analytics, to see which countries use the app. Not linked to you.
  • Crash reports (the state of the app when it crashed) to Google Firebase Crashlytics, to find and fix crashes. Not linked to you.
  • A request for app configuration, such as whether a newer version is available, to Google Firebase Remote Config, to show update prompts.

None of this includes the contents of your lists, notes or photos. Google processes it for us as a service provider; see Firebase's privacy information.

How long it is kept

Analytics event data is kept for 2 months. Analytics data tied to an app installation is kept for up to 14 months, and that period restarts each time the app is used. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

What we never do

  • Sell your data, or share it with anyone other than the service provider above, except where the law requires it.
  • Show ads, use your advertising identifier, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Ask for an account, a name or an email address.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The data described above is anonymous and the same for every user.

Your choices

Deleting the app stops all analytics and crash reporting. Because the data isn't linked to your identity, we can't look up or delete one person's records, but you can always write to us with a question.

Feedback

If you send us feedback in Mochi List (through the feedback button, form or app screen), we receive your message, the kind of feedback you chose, an optional star rating, the page or app version you were using, and your language setting. We ask for your email only if you want a reply, and we keep it only if you tick the box allowing us to contact you; we delete it after at most 90 days. We don't use cookies or tracking for feedback, and we don't collect device identifiers. We also store the IP address your feedback was sent from, to prevent abuse; it is deleted with your feedback after one year. Feedback is stored by PTK Studio's 88eggs service (hosted in the United States), used only to fix problems and improve Mochi List, never sold or shared, and deleted after one year. To have your feedback deleted sooner, contact us at hello@ptk-studio.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change affects what the app sends (for example a future sign-in or sharing feature), this page is updated before that version of the app is released.
