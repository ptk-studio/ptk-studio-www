Mochi List

Support

Mochi List turns one list into many ways to use it: a checklist, a spin wheel, a randomizer, flash cards, or a plain list. If something isn't behaving, start here, and if the answer isn't below, write to us.

Common questions

  • Where are my lists stored?

    On your device only. There's no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your lists. To keep a copy, open the list's Advanced Actions and choose Export List; it saves a .zip package.

  • How do I move lists to a new phone?

    Export each list (Export List), send the .zip to the new device (AirDrop, Messages, Files), then use Import in Mochi List and pick the file.

  • Does the app need the internet?

    No. Everything works offline. When online, the app sends anonymous usage and crash data (see the privacy policy).

  • How do I reset a checklist?

    Open the list in Checklist mode, tap Reset All, then confirm Reset.

  • Which version am I running?

    iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Mochi List shows it.

Still stuck

Write to support@mochi-factory.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version and the app version.
