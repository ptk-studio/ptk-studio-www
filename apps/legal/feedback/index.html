What helps

For the content — the most useful message you can send — the area, the situation and the topic, and the sentence itself, quoted. Every substantive statement is linked to the official page it came from, so if that page now says something different we can check it against the source and correct it in a content update. Court procedures change more often than the laws behind them do.

If the problem is that a topic describes your state badly, say which state. There is no jurisdiction picker on purpose, and the fix is usually to sharpen how much a situation says it varies, or to point at a better page on that state's court site — not to add a rule.

For a bug, your iOS version and the app version from Settings → About, and what you were doing when it happened. If something is unreadable or unreachable with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type or Reduce Motion turned on, that is a bug too, and worth reporting the same way.

Please leave your own legal matter out — no documents, no details. We cannot advise on them, and the app is built specifically so that nobody here ever holds anything about your situation. If you are reading this on a shared phone, note that App Lock, Hide in app switcher and the Leave button do not cover an email you have sent.

What we cannot do

We cannot tell you what to do about your situation, predict what a court will decide, work out a support amount or a property share, or say whether you qualify for anything. Writing to us creates no attorney-client relationship. Those questions need a licensed attorney or your court's self-help centre, which the app points at throughout and which costs nothing.

If you need help now, the app's first screen links to national safety and legal-aid resources, including domestic violence support. Those are staffed. This inbox is not.
