Worldly

Every country in the world, in your pocket.

Capitals, populations, heads of state, public holidays and the history behind them — for 197 countries and territories. The whole reference ships inside the app, so it works on a plane, on a train, and in the middle of nowhere.

What it is

  • A reference that works without signal

    Nothing is fetched while you read. Every country, holiday and date is stored on the device, so the app is exactly as useful at 38,000 feet as it is at home.

  • Search that knows capitals too

    Type a country, a capital, a region or a country code. Accents and capitalisation don't matter — turkiye finds Türkiye, Nairobi finds Kenya.

  • Five ways into each country

    An overview of geography and people, the government and who currently leads it, the public holidays and what they mark, a general history with a dated timeline, and the odd facts worth knowing.

  • Keep the ones you care about

    Bookmark a country and it lands in its own tab, searchable, ready for the trip you're planning. Bookmarks stay on your device.

  • What isn't here

    No ads. No account, no sign-up, no password. Nothing nagging you to come back, and no paywall in front of the reference.

About the data

Figures were compiled as of May 2026 and cover the 193 UN member states, Vatican City, Palestine, Taiwan and Hong Kong. Populations are estimates and office-holders change; the app shows the date the data was compiled so you always know how fresh it is.
