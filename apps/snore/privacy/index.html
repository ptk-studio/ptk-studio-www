Mochi Snore

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

The short version. Snore listens while you sleep and works out what it heard on the phone itself. The recordings never leave your device — there is no upload path for audio in the app at all. There is no account and no sign-up. The app does report anonymous usage counts, described in full below, and because there is no account they carry no name and no email.

Who we are

Mochi Snore is made by PTK Studio. This policy covers the Mochi Snore iOS app and this page. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@ptk-studio.com and we'll answer plainly.

What stays on your device

Everything about your nights:

  • The audio. Recordings are written into the app's own storage, marked so they are excluded from iCloud backup, and deleted along with the night they belong to.
  • The snores detected in each night — when each one happened, how long it lasted and how loud it was.
  • Your score, timeline and hour-by-hour breakdown, all of which are worked out on the phone from the night's own measurements.
  • The factors you tag a night with, including any factor you name yourself.
  • Your settings — sensitivity, audio quality, calibration.

We have no copy of any of it and we cannot read it. Deleting a night deletes its audio. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app reports

Snore uses Firebase Analytics to count how the app is used, so we can tell which parts are worth improving. It reports named product events whose values are counts, durations in whole minutes, and fixed options — for example that a recording was saved, roughly how many snores it contained, how many minutes it ran, and which screens were opened.

Two identifiers come with that:

  • A random identifier the app generates on first launch. It is not derived from you or your device — reinstalling the app produces a new one, and two devices count as two different users.
  • An app instance identifier that the analytics SDK maintains on its own.

Because there is no account, none of this is attached to a name, an email address or a person. We can see that some anonymous installation recorded a night with forty snores. We cannot see whose it was.

What is never reported

  • Audio, in any form. No recording, no fragment of one, and no file name. The app has no upload path for audio.
  • Anything you type. The one free-text field in the app — a factor you name yourself — is reported as the literal word "custom", never as what you wrote.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account — there is no sign-up.
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, camera, or location.
  • Your advertising identifier. The app deliberately uses the build of the analytics SDK that has no advertising-identifier support, so there is nothing for it to link. You will never see an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because the app does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Health data. Snore does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Permissions, and when they are asked for

Snore asks for one thing:

  • The microphone — requested the first time you start a recording, because listening is the entire app. iOS shows this prompt itself. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

The app also declares the background audio mode, which is what lets a recording keep running with the phone locked and the screen off. It is not a permission you are asked for and it grants no access to anything else.

Who else touches it

Google, as the provider of Firebase Analytics, processes the anonymous usage counts described above on our behalf. Nothing else in the app talks to anyone: there is no advertising network, no crash reporter, and no server of ours. No data of yours is sold, and none of it is combined with data from other companies.

Children

Mochi Snore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. There is no chat, no user-to-user messaging, and no public content in the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Record without keeping audio. Turn audio saving off in Settings and the app measures the night without writing a recording at all.
  • Delete a night from the Snores list, which removes its statistics and its audio together.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app.
  • Ask us anything at hello@ptk-studio.com. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and we honour those requests regardless of where you live. In Snore's case the honest answer is that the part you care about — your recordings and your nights — never reached us to begin with.

Not a medical device

Snore does not diagnose anything and does not detect sleep apnoea. Loud, frequent snoring with pauses in breathing can be a sign of it, and that is worth raising with a doctor rather than with an app. Nothing the app shows you is a medical finding, and the score is a way of comparing your own nights to each other.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we'll update this page and change the date at the top before the change ships. Material changes will also be noted in the release notes.
