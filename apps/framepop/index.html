Photos in, video out — in about a minute, with no editing.

Pick a template. Drop your photos in. The template already chose the timing, the motion, the transitions and the look, so the first thing you see is already worth sending.

What it does

  • Photos that share the screen

    Most slideshow apps show your pictures one after another. FramePop also builds them into one picture — a photo booth strip, a passport-style grid, a pile of Polaroids, a fanned hand of cards. Each photo gets its moment, then the whole thing lights up together.

  • Templates that move

    Confetti that bursts, hearts that rise, words that type themselves on screen. Every template is a look somebody designed, not a filter you have to configure.

  • Cut the background out

    Some templates lift the subject out of the photo and stand them on the design, so the video looks made rather than assembled.

  • Yours, if you want it to be

    Add your own words and emoji, and say where they sit and which photo they belong to. Change the background, the transition, the length, the music — or change nothing at all, which is the point.

  • No watermark. No subscription.

    Nothing is stamped on your video and nothing renews.

  • On your device

    No account, no sign-in. Your photos are read when you pick them, and everything FramePop makes stays on your phone until you share it — nothing you make is ever uploaded.
