Mochi Gym

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version. There’s no account and no sign-in. Your workouts, sets and routines are stored on your phone and nowhere else. We do collect anonymous usage counts and crash reports, and this page says exactly what those are.

Who we are

Mochi Gym is made by Mochi Factory, run by PTK Studio. This policy covers the Mochi Gym iOS app and this page. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@mochi-factory.com and we’ll give you a plain answer.

What you tell the app

Only your training: the exercises, sets, reps, weights and times you log, and the routines you build. There’s no sign-up and no profile. The app never asks for your name, email, age, weight or anything about your health beyond the sets you choose to enter.

What stays on your device

  • Your workout history: sessions, sets, reps, weights and durations.
  • Your routines.
  • Your settings: weight unit, rest timer, haptics, keep screen awake, and appearance.

These live in the app’s own storage. We have no copy of them and can’t read them. Settings → Your Data → Clear All Workout History deletes your log, and deleting the app deletes everything. The muscle map is calculated on your phone from that log, and the result is never sent anywhere.

What we collect

Anonymous usage counts and crash reports, through two services, and nothing that identifies you:

  • Firebase Analytics, from Google: counts of app opens and of actions such as viewing a muscle or exercise, starting or finishing a workout, logging a set, and changing the weight unit. It also records the device model, iOS version and the country your connection appears to come from. The app sends a random identifier made for this installation, not for you. It doesn’t use Apple’s advertising identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics, from Google: if the app crashes, a report of where in the code it failed, with the device model, iOS version and app version, so we can fix it.

These counts tell us which parts of the app get used, so the next version improves the parts that matter. They can say which built-in exercise you logged a set for, but never the reps, the time or the weight, the names you give your routines, or anything you type.

You can turn this off. Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage stops both Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Turning it off also deletes this installation’s analytics data and its random identifier. It’s on when you first install the app. Crash reporting stops from the next time you open the app.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account. There’s no sign-up.
  • Your location, photos, contacts or Apple Health data. The app doesn’t ask for them and can’t read them.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Sharing

We don’t sell or share data. Google processes the anonymous counts and crash reports above on our behalf.

Children

Mochi Gym isn’t directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date at the top.
