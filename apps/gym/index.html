Mochi Gym

A training log that shows you your body.

Mochi Gym puts your training on an anatomy figure. Every muscle you’ve worked lights up, and the ones you’ve been skipping stay cool. Tap a muscle to see its exercises, start one and log the set. The map updates as you go.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

What it is

  • The muscle map

    Each muscle shows as trained (in the last 2 days), recovering or neglected, worked out from the sets you’ve logged over a window you choose: 7, 14 or 30 days.

  • A nudge, not a nag

    Muscles you haven’t trained in a while are marked. Tap one to see what works it.

  • Log it your way

    Reps and weight, or time for holds and planks. Kilograms or pounds. A rest timer that can start itself after each set.

  • About 60 exercises

    Grouped by muscle, each with how to do it.

  • Routines and Quick Workout

    Build routines or just start a quick workout, with a history of every session.

  • Try it before you train

    “Show a sample week” fills the map with clearly labelled demo data, which is never mixed into your own log.

  • Made for everyone

    VoiceOver reads each muscle’s state, and the app works at the largest text sizes.

  • No account, no subscription

    Your log lives on your phone.
