Mochi Gym

Support

Mochi Gym is a training log built around a map of your muscles. If something isn’t working, start here. If your answer isn’t below, write to us.

Common questions

  • Does it work without a connection?

    Yes, all of it. The exercises, your log and the muscle map are all on your phone, so it works in a basement gym with no signal.

  • How does the map decide what’s trained, recovering or neglected?

    From the last time you logged a set that worked that muscle: within 2 days is trained, 3 to 6 days is recovering, and a week or more (or never) is neglected. The weekly figures count the last 7 days. A set counts in full for the exercise’s main muscle and as half a set for the muscles that assist.

  • What is the sample week?

    Demo data that shows what a filled-in map looks like. It’s labelled as a sample, it isn’t saved, and it never mixes with your own workouts. Switch it off and your own log is back.

  • Kilograms or pounds?

    Settings → Units → Weight.

  • Can I log planks and other holds?

    Yes. Holds are logged by time instead of reps and weight.

  • Can the rest timer start by itself?

    Yes: Settings → “Auto-start after a set”.

  • How do I delete my data?

    Settings → Your Data → Clear All Workout History. Deleting the app removes the rest. There’s no account to close.

  • Is this medical or coaching advice?

    No. Mochi Gym is a log. Check with a professional before starting a new training programme.

  • What do you collect about me?

    Nothing that identifies you, and never your sets: no reps, times or weights. We count app opens and actions to see which parts get used. The privacy policy names both services and exactly what each one receives. To stop even that, turn off Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage.

Still stuck

Email hello@ptk-studio.com, or use Send feedback in the app. Tell us your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version and the screen you were on. Those details help most.
