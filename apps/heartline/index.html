Mochi Heartline

One kind message at a time.

Open it and there's a single card waiting — something warm, something proud of you, something that says the thing you needed to hear today. Swipe for another. Tap the heart to keep the ones that land.

What it is

  • A deck you play with

    One message, full screen, breathing gently. Swipe right for the next, left to go back, or tap the heart to save it. Every message carries its own illustration and a tone.

  • Keep what matters

    The messages you save collect in Saved, searchable, waiting for the day you need to read them again.

  • On your home screen

    Add the widget and the latest message sits quietly there, no app-opening required.

  • Four looks, four moods

    Cozy Heart, French Kiss, Sakura Pop and Lantern Glow each change the colours, the typography and the way the card moves, and bring their own gentle background music.

  • What isn't here

    No ads. No account, no sign-up, no password. No streaks, no guilt, and nothing nagging you to come back.
