Mochi Trip

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

The short version. There is no account and no sign-in, and we run no server of our own. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, your itinerary is written on the device and nothing you type leaves it. On iPhones that cannot run Apple Intelligence, the trip details you enter are sent to Google to write the plan. Which of those applies is shown in the app, under Settings.

Who we are

Mochi Trip is made by PTK Studio. This policy covers the Mochi Trip iOS app and this page. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@ptk-studio.com and we'll answer plainly.

What you tell the app

To write a plan, Trip asks for a destination, how many days, what the trip is for, who is travelling, a budget, and an optional notes field you can type anything into. That is the whole of what it asks for. There is no name, no email, no account.

Where the plan is written

On your iPhone, where it can be. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model writes the itinerary. Nothing you typed is transmitted, and the app works with the phone in aeroplane mode.

Through Google, where it cannot. Older iPhones cannot run Apple Intelligence. On those, the plan is written by a Gemini model reached through Firebase AI Logic, and the details above — including anything you wrote in the notes field — are sent to Google in order to produce it. Google's handling of that request is governed by their terms; we do not receive a copy and we keep no record of it.

The app tells you which one is in use. Settings names the model and says, in the same words, whether your details are being sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • The plans you save.
  • A cache of plans already generated, so asking for the same trip twice doesn't write it twice.
  • A local count of plans written this month, and — where the hosted model is used — a running estimate of what it cost.
  • Your settings.

These live in the app's own storage. We have no copy of them and cannot read them. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also delete saved trips individually, or all at once, in Settings.

What we collect

Nothing. Trip sends no analytics, no crash reports and no usage data to us. We operate no server, so there is nowhere for anything to arrive.

If that ever changes, this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account — there is no sign-up.
  • Your location. Trip never asks for it, and cannot read it.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, or microphone.
  • Advertising identifiers. Trip shows no ads and is not part of any ad network.

Who else touches it

Google, and only on devices that cannot run Apple Intelligence, for the reason described above. The request is made through Firebase AI Logic, which also runs a check that the request came from a genuine copy of the app rather than something impersonating it.

Two more are worth naming because they are yours, not ours. Tapping Maps next to a place hands Apple Maps a search for that name — Trip sends no location and learns nothing about what you do there. Tapping Share hands the plan's text to whichever app or person you pick, and their privacy policy takes over at that point.

Children

Trip is not directed at children and collects nothing that would identify anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the page is updated before the version of the app that made the change is released — not after.
