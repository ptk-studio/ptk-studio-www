Mochi Trip

Answer five questions, get a day-by-day itinerary. Written on your iPhone, no account needed.

Tell Trip where you're going, how long for, what the trip is for, who's coming and roughly what you want to spend. It plans the days — what to see and when, how long to allow, roughly what it costs, and where to eat. Each day stays in one neighbourhood, so you're not crossing the city twice to see two things.

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence the whole thing is written on the device. No account, no sign-up, and nothing you type leaves your phone.
