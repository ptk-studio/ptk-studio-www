What helps

For a state fact that reads wrongly, the state and which of the eight it is — residency, waiting period, living apart, property system, fault grounds, filing fee, fee waiver, or what is public. Each one names the section it was written from, so telling us which section you checked it against is the fastest route to a correction.

For a figure, whether it was marked set by law or a planning assumption. Those come from different places and get corrected differently: one is a citation to fix, the other is a model to argue with. Tapping the timeline shows every input that produced its range.

For a bug, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Please leave out anything about your own case. The app has nowhere to type a name for the same reason, and an email is far less private than the encrypted store on your phone.

What we cannot do

We cannot tell you whether to end a marriage, what a court would decide, what you are owed, or whether a deadline is yours. That needs someone who knows your state and your facts and is accountable for the answer — a licensed attorney, or your court's self-help centre, which the app points at throughout and which costs nothing.

If you are not safe, the first screen of the app has a route straight to emergency numbers, the National Domestic Violence Hotline and the 988 Lifeline, with a quick exit. Please use it rather than this page.
