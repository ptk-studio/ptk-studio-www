Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

The short version. Nothing leaves your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising identifier. There is no network client in the app at all, so there is nothing for it to send and nowhere for it to send it. The app asks five questions so its figures mean something, and keeps the answers in the encrypted store on your device.

Who we are

Mochi Divorce Legal is made by PTK Studio. This policy covers the Mochi Divorce Legal iOS app and this page. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@ptk-studio.com and we will answer plainly.

What we collect

Nothing. The app declares no third-party packages, links no SDKs, and contains no networking code. It never contacts a server of ours — we do not run one. We do not know which state you chose, which pages you read, whether you saved anything, or whether you opened the app at all.

Because we hold nothing about you, there is nothing for us to sell, share with data brokers, or use to advertise to you. There are no ads in the app.

The app's automated tests fail the build if an import capable of opening a network connection ever appears in it. That is how this promise is kept true rather than merely intended.

What stays on your device

  • Your answers to five questions — which state, whether there are children under 18, how much the two of you agree, what there is to divide, and roughly how long you have been married. Every one is optional and every one can be cleared.
  • Any hourly rate or retainer you typed into the cost worksheet.
  • The pages you bookmarked, stored as page identifiers that ship inside the app.

These live in the app's own storage, created with iOS file protection so the file is unreadable while your device is locked. We have no copy and cannot read them. Deleting the app deletes them, and Delete everything in Settings removes them immediately without uninstalling.

There is nowhere to type a name

This is worth stating plainly, because it is unusual. The app has no free text anywhere: no notes, no documents, no case numbers, no party names. There is no field in which anyone could enter a name, an address or an account number, because no such field exists. The five answers are stored as fixed choices and a two-letter state code.

Opening official pages

The app links to courts, state legislatures and federal agencies throughout. Those open in an in-app Safari view, which runs in its own process: it shows you the real web address, and the app has no access to that browsing, its history, its cookies or its autofill data. Where you tap a telephone number — a domestic violence hotline, for instance — it goes to your dialler rather than to a web page.

Those sites are not ours and have their own privacy policies. We are not told that you visited one.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. Everything the app stores is on your own device and under your control, and Settings will delete all of it in one step.

Changes

If the app ever collects anything, this page, the wording inside the app, and its App Store privacy disclosure will all change before that version ships — not after.

Not legal advice

Mochi Divorce Legal provides general legal information. It is not legal advice, it does not create a solicitor-client or attorney-client relationship, and it is not a substitute for a licensed attorney or a court self-help centre.
