Mochi FramePop

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

The short version. FramePop collects nothing about you. There is no account and no sign-in, your photos are read only when you choose them, and nothing you make is uploaded anywhere. Your videos stay on your device until you decide to share them.

Who we are

Mochi FramePop is made by PTK Studio. This policy covers the Mochi FramePop iOS app and this page. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@ptk-studio.com and we'll answer plainly.

Photos

FramePop reads a photo only when you hand it one — through the system photo picker, or from the camera when you take a picture inside the app. It never browses your library on its own.

A chosen photo is held in the app while you work, and used to render your video on the device. Some templates ask iOS to separate the subject from the background; that happens on your phone, using Apple's on-device Vision framework, and the result never leaves it. No photo, and nothing derived from a photo, is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • The videos you make, and the poster frames shown in the Videos tab.
  • Which templates you've liked, and which packs you've added.
  • Your settings.

These live in the app's own storage. We have no copy of them and cannot read them. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also delete videos individually, or all at once, in Settings.

What we collect

Nothing. The current version of FramePop sends no analytics, no crash reports and no usage data. It makes no network requests of its own at all — the app works the same with the phone in aeroplane mode.

If that ever changes, this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released, and anything collected would be limited to facts about the app — which template was opened, whether an export succeeded — never your photos, the words you type, or the names of your files.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account — there is no sign-up.
  • Your photos, or anything derived from them.
  • The words you type into a template.
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone, or location.
  • Advertising identifiers. FramePop shows no ads and is not part of any ad network.

Who else touches it

Nobody. There is no analytics provider, no advertising partner, and no server of ours for anything to be sent to.

Two things are worth naming because they are yours, not ours. When you tap Share, iOS hands the video to whichever app or person you pick, and their policy applies from that point. And if you turn on Save to Photos automatically, a copy of your video goes into your own photo library, where iCloud Photos may sync it under your own Apple account settings.

How long we keep it

We hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to keep. What is on your device stays until you remove it.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. You can revoke FramePop's access to your photos at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos; the app still works, and will ask again the next time you pick a picture.

Children

FramePop is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what the app collects will be published here before the app that makes it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything else, go to hello@ptk-studio.com.
