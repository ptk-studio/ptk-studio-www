Mochi Heartline

Support

A small, warm app that hands you one kind message at a time. If something isn't working, or you just want to tell us what you think, there's a real person at the other end of both links below.

Common questions

  • I'm not hearing any music.

    Music is off until you ask for it. Open Settings → Sounds and turn it on, and each experience will play its own looping track. If it's already on and still silent, check the ring/silent switch on the side of your phone — the music deliberately respects it, so a muted phone stays quiet.

  • Where do my saved messages go?

    Onto your device, and nowhere else. Tap the heart on any message and it appears under Saved, where you can search back through them. Because they're stored only on your phone, deleting the app deletes them too.

  • The home-screen widget isn't changing.

    The widget shows the most recent message the app has handed you, so it refreshes when you open the app and draw a new one. Opening Heartline for a moment will bring the widget back in step. If it still looks stuck, remove the widget and add it again.

  • I've seen every message already.

    Right now the deck keeps drawing rather than stopping — a good message is meant to be re-read, and an affirmation doesn't expire. We're actively writing more, and a much larger library is the next thing we're shipping.

  • Some packs and experiences say "Soon".

    Those aren't available yet. Nothing in the app is behind a payment today — everything you can open, you already own.

  • Can I change how the app looks?

    Yes. Settings → Experience lets you switch between the bundled looks. Each one changes the colours, the typography, the way the card moves, and its music.
