Printable School Year Calendar 2027-2028 in 2 Minutes

November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

You need a calendar that runs from August 2027 to June 2028, with the days off already marked, and you would like it on paper today. This post walks through a way to get it printed in about two minutes, plus a second route that needs no special tools.

Step 1: Gather your school's dates (30 seconds)

The calendar is only as good as the dates you put on it. Before you open anything, find your school or district calendar, which is usually a PDF on the school website or in a back-to-school email. Pull out four things:

  • The first and last day of school.
  • Breaks, such as fall, winter and spring break.
  • Single days off, such as teacher workdays and conference days.
  • Any early-release days you want to remember.

Keep that list open in another tab or on a scrap of paper. School-specific dates differ from one district to the next, so copy them from your school rather than from a general holiday list.

Option B: No special tools

If you would rather not use any online tool, there are two plain routes.

  • Print the school's own calendar. Most districts publish a PDF. Print it as is, then highlight the breaks and teacher workdays with a marker. This is the quickest option if the district version is already readable.
  • Use a word processor or spreadsheet template. Both Microsoft Word and Google Docs offer calendar templates, and a spreadsheet works too. Make one tab or page for each month, type in your school's dates, and shade the days off with a fill color. It takes longer than two minutes the first time, but you can reuse it every year.

Either route is fine. The main thing is to start from your school's official dates.

Print it without surprises

A calendar that prints at the wrong size is annoying, and most of the trouble comes from a few print settings.

  • Scale: set it to 100%, not "fit to page". Shrinking changes the size of the boxes and the text.
  • Margins: if you are printing from a browser calendar maker, choose None, since the page already includes its own spacing.
  • Paper: make sure the paper size in the print dialog matches the size you chose when you made the calendar.
  • Black and white: weekend shading and highlighted days still read well in grayscale, so you do not need color ink.

If you would like a PDF instead of paper, choose Print, then Save as PDF as the destination, keeping scale at 100% and margins at None. Make it more useful once it is printed

A few small habits keep a school year calendar working past September:

  • Put it where everyone passes, such as the fridge, a hallway wall or the front of a binder.
  • Mark the first day of each break in a bold color, so the gaps in childcare or work are easy to see at a glance.
  • Leave a few days of the early months blank. Districts sometimes change a date, and a pencil is easier than reprinting.
  • Check the school website again in the spring. If dates shift for weather make-up days, you will want to catch it.

Quick checklist

  • School or district calendar open, with breaks and single days off copied down.
  • Months set from August 2027 to June 2028, which is 11 months.
  • Layout chosen: one month per page, or 2, 4 or 6 per sheet.
  • Paper size matches your printer paper.
  • School days off added or highlighted.
  • Print scale at 100%, margins at None for browser-made pages.
  • Test page checked before printing every sheet.
